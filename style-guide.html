AfterImpact design language

Decided system: the brand personality (calm, competent, reassuring — DQ-1), light and dark themes following the OS preference with a manual override (DQ-3), logo.svg as the only mark (DQ-4), the system font stack (DQ-5), and the responsive breakpoints are final in DESIGN.md. This page is its rendered reference.

Brand and logo

The two paths bracket a central transition and continue forward after a disruptive event. Use the mark at 24px or larger, preserve one-eighth of its width as clear space, and keep it on an uncluttered background or surface; in each theme its paths render with that theme's primary and secondary values. The product name typeset in the primary UI family is the interim wordmark; a designed lockup is post-v1.

Color tokens

Both themes ship; clients follow the OS preference with a manual override. Tokens keep their names and roles across themes — components reference tokens, never raw hex values.
